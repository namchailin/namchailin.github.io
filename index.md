## Biography

<img class="profile-picture" src="profile.jpg" alt="Chailin Nam">
<a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=w4oIHI0AAAAJ&amp;hl=ko">Chailin Nam</a> is an M.S. student in the <a href="https://sw.ssu.ac.kr/">School of Software at Soongsil University</a>, where she conducts research at the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/ssu-nlp/home">Natural Language Processing Lab</a>. Her research interests lie in natural language processing (NLP) and large language models (LLMs), with a focus on developing autonomous agents that effectively understand and interact with humans. For more details, please see her <a href="./chailin_CV.pdf">CV</a>.
